## 📋 Crypto Twitter best practices

- **Post during US peak hours** for memecoin engagement: roughly 13:00–16:00 WIB (US morning) or 22:00–02:00 WIB (US evening).
- **Engage on every reply** in the first hour — Twitter algo rewards conversations.
- **Pin the launch tweet** the moment CA is live.
- **Quote-tweet your own previous lore** to build a thread of canon.
- **Tag sparingly**: only when relevant. Tag-spam looks desperate.
- **Use 1-2 hashtags max** per tweet — `$NADKING` ticker + `#NADKING` or `#Monad` is enough.
- **Reply to bigger Monad accounts** with on-brand King personality. Be the funny one in their replies, not the begging one.

---

## 🛠 How to use the image prompts

### ChatGPT (DALL-E 3)
Paste the entire prompt block (anchor + scene). DALL-E 3 reads the whole thing well and stays consistent if you repeat the anchor each time.

### Midjourney
Paste the prompt, then add `--ar 16:9 --v 6 --style raw` at the end (or `--ar 1:1` for square posts).

### Sora / Runway / Kling (for video later)
Use the image prompt as a starting frame description, then add motion: e.g. *"...camera slowly pushes in, mascot's cape ripples in slow motion, gold particles drift upward."*
